## Notes

- Icon data for Phosphor, Tabler, Solar, IconPark, Fluent UI Color, Health Icons, Streamline and Fluent Emoji Flat was taken from the Iconify JSON packages (`@iconify-json/*`), which carry the upstream set's own license.
- Brand and logo icons were left out of every set (trademarks).
- Fluent Emoji 3D: 387 slide-related emoji from microsoft/fluentui-emoji commit 1ffb34c7, resized from 256px PNG to 128px WebP.
- MIT and Apache-2.0 sets need no per-slide credit; keep their license text reachable from the credits page.
